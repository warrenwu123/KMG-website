About Matsu

Matsu is a satellite iron ore deposit of KMG's Ridges Iron Ore Project in the East Kimberley, lying about 10 km south-southeast of the Ridges mine. It holds several million tonnes of high-grade direct shipping ore (DSO).

Mining leases and associated tenements were granted in 2014, when the proposal was also referred to the WA Environmental Protection Authority. The project is well advanced, and KMG is preparing the submissions needed to start mining, with timing subject to iron ore prices, market conditions and exchange rates.
