Who we are

Iron ore from the Kimberley to the world

Kimberley Metals Group (KMG) is an active explorer, developer and operator of iron ore projects in Australia. Since 2011 we have built a business that runs from pit to ship, and we own the Ridges and Matsu Iron Ore Projects in the Far North region of Western Australia.

We handle production, grading, processing and global logistics in-house. Ore is hauled to the Port of Wyndham, a captive port developed and controlled by KMG and its affiliates, and sold through strong marketing relationships with key end-users in the industry.

KMG is a significant employer in Western Australia's mining industry, and we are committed to the safety and wellbeing of our people, the environment and the communities where we operate.
