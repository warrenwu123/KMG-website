How we operate

Pit to port, under one roof

We control every link in the chain — from the ore body to the ship's hold — through infrastructure KMG and its affiliates built and run.

01

Mine

High-grade direct shipping ore from the Ridges and Matsu deposits in the East Kimberley.

02

Haul

Road haulage along the Great Northern Highway — 165 km from the Ridges to the coast.

03

Ship

Export through the Port of Wyndham, developed and controlled by KMG and its affiliates.
