About Tamreas Copperfields

Through KMG Chile, Kimberley Metals Group holds around 6,000 hectares across the Copiapó and Valparaíso regions. The ground hosts traditional copper and iron deposits, along with gold and molybdenum, and sits near major producers and a sea port.

KMG's aim is to develop Tamreas Copperfields into world-class gold and copper mines, serving demand it expects to continue for decades.

Alicahue flagship

  • More than 2,000 hectares, worked for copper until 2022
  • Copper ore was sold to local smelters at an average grade of 2–3%
  • An existing 180 m tunnel, plus six tunnels planned to be joined
  • Six further hills being explored for gold and copper, with molybdenum

Development plan

  • Full-scale development of the existing tunnels
  • Excavation of newly found pits
  • Ramp development to resume copper ore extraction
  • Detailed exploration, including LiDAR surveys, from April 2025
  • Goal: a fully JORC-compliant resource
