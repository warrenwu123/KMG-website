About Halls Creek

The Halls Creek Gold Project (HCP) lies east of the town of Halls Creek in the Kimberley Region of Western Australia. It brings together three main gold projects (Nicolson, Grants Creek and Mary River) across a large and relatively underexplored land package.

The project has historically produced 300 koz of gold and holds existing underground and open pit Ore Reserves. All orebodies remain open at depth, with multiple drill-tested targets and significant greenfield potential.

Development opportunities

  • Open pit: a 2–3 Mt exploration target could support a mill expansion to treat lower-grade, lower-cost open pit ore, with near-mine targets already identified from drilling data
  • Underground: significant capital development is in place, and three levels of ore at Wagtail South can be extracted with no further capex
  • High strip-ratio open pit ore can be reached through existing underground infrastructure by incline development
  • Mary River: a greenfield opportunity backed by historical mining and recent drilling

Infrastructure on site

  • 250 ktpa processing plant, last operated in June 2023 and kept in a state of operational readiness
  • Tailings storage facilities TSF1 and TSF2, with about 6–8 months' capacity and scope for a further lift
  • Power from a third-party-operated power station on the tenure
  • Process water from storm water harvesting and existing bore fields
