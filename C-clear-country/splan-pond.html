About Splan Pond

Splan Pond sits in a historic nickel region of New Brunswick, about 20 miles from the United States border. Studies to date indicate a strong presence of rare earth elements, lithium and nickel.

Geotechnical studies have been completed, with more underway. The project's strategic location and potential have drawn interest from a number of large exploration companies.

Work to date

  • Geotechnical studies completed
  • Further studies underway
  • Two main target areas identified
